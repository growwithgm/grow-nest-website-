Shipping & returns

Shipping and returns for ecommerce

Our work doesn't stop at checkout. We set up rates, labels and tracking so every parcel arrives, and we warn you before one comes back.

Get a shipping proposal WhatsApp

What we do in logistics

  • Carrier rates and weight-based shipping zones (FedEx, Correos)
  • Shipping calculators and free-shipping bars by country
  • Label generation and bulk shipping files
  • Daily tracking of every order, with alerts for stuck parcels
  • Reminders that get customers to collect parcels before they're returned
  • A warehouse scanning app for outgoing and returned parcels

Fewer returns, more delivered orders

Many parcels come back because the customer wasn't home or doesn't know the order is waiting at the post office. Our tracking spots these cases in time and sends the customer the pickup address.
