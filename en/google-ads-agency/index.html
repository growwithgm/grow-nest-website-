FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Google Shopping?

The ads with a photo, price and store name that appear when you search for a product. They come from your product feed in Merchant Center.

Google Ads or Meta Ads?

Google reaches people already searching for your product. Meta creates demand among people who aren't searching yet. Most stores need both.

How long does Google Ads take to work?

First data arrives in 7 to 14 days. By around 30 days you can see which campaigns deserve more budget.
