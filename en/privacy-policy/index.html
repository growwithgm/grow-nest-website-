Privacy policy

This page explains what data we collect when you contact us and what we use it for.

Who handles your data

Grow Nest. Contact: connect.grownest@gmail.com.

What we collect

What you type into the contact form: name, email, WhatsApp (optional), your store's website, the services you're interested in and your message. If you write to us on WhatsApp or by email, also your number or email address.

What we use it for

Only to reply to your request and, if we work together, to run the service. We don't sell it or use it to send you advertising without your permission.

Who else sees it

The form is sent to our inbox through the FormSubmit service. Our email runs on Google.

How long we keep it

For as long as the conversation or the service lasts, and afterwards for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or limit how it's used, by writing to connect.grownest@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

Cookies

This site uses no advertising or analytics cookies of its own.
