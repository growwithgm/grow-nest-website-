FAQ

Facebook and Meta Ads: frequently asked questions

What does a Facebook Ads agency do?

It plans, creates and optimises your Facebook and Instagram ads so they sell more on the same budget. At Grow Nest we also set up tracking, the product catalogue and the creatives.

Do you only manage the ads?

No. We handle everything from website to sales: we build or improve your store, run Meta Ads and Google Ads, optimise the pages, set up tracking and look after messages and shipping.

Are Facebook Ads and Meta Ads the same thing?

Yes. Meta Ads is the current name of the platform that runs your ads on Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and Meta's other apps.

How much does it cost to advertise on Facebook and Instagram?

Meta has no fixed price. You set a daily budget and Meta charges for impressions or clicks. In the audit we recommend a budget based on your margin and goals.

How much does Grow Nest charge to manage Meta Ads?

It depends on the markets, campaigns and creatives you need. We give you a fixed quote after the audit.

How soon will I see results?

First data arrives in 7 to 14 days. By around 30 days it's clear what works.

Do you only work with stores in Spain?

No. We run campaigns across Europe, the UK, the US, the Gulf, Australia and other markets.

Is there a minimum contract?

No. After the setup period, the service runs month to month.

Do you guarantee sales?

No. Advertising means testing and learning. We guarantee the work, clear reporting and honest advice about what is and isn't working.
