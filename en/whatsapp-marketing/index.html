How we recover an abandoned cart

  1. The customer leaves products in the cart without paying.
  2. They get a WhatsApp message with their cart and a direct link to finish buying.
  3. If they don't buy, a second reminder arrives later.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the WhatsApp Business API?

The official version of WhatsApp for businesses. It sends automatic messages from your store using templates approved by Meta.

Can I message all my customers on WhatsApp?

Only those who have given consent. We help you collect it at checkout and in your forms.

Does it work with Shopify?

Yes. We connect WhatsApp to your store's events: cart, order, shipping and delivery.
