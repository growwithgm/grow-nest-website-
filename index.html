What we build and run

Shopify Development

Stores that load fast and sell on every phone.

New stores, redesigns and custom themes on Shopify, and WooCommerce when a brand already runs on it. We write our own sections instead of stacking apps, so the store stays fast and easy to edit.

  • New store builds and redesigns
  • Custom themes and Liquid sections
  • Markets, currencies, taxes and shipping zones
  • Google Merchant Center and product feeds
  • Shopify Flow, Forms, and Order Printer invoices and packing slips
  • Speed, mobile and checkout fixes

B2B Wholesale

Sell wholesale from the same store.

We set up native Shopify B2B, so trade buyers log in, see their own prices and order in bulk while retail shoppers see the normal store. Then we build the system that brings new wholesale buyers in.

  • Company accounts, price lists and quantity-tier pricing
  • Wholesale-only products and separate B2B shipping rules
  • Wholesale landing pages that capture trade leads
  • A lead CRM with pipeline stages and WhatsApp and email follow-up
  • Outreach to salons, boutiques and retailers

Ads & Tracking

Ads that reach real buyers, measured on real orders.

Google and Meta campaigns built on clean tracking, so spend is judged by orders in your store, not by platform estimates.

  • Google Shopping, Search, Display and YouTube
  • Meta Ads: prospecting, catalog ads and retargeting
  • Campaigns across several countries (UK, Spain, Germany, France, Netherlands, Gulf)
  • Regular search-term cleanup and negative keyword lists
  • GA4, Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel and Conversions API, TikTok Pixel
  • One reporting dashboard for every ad platform

WhatsApp Automation

How we work

From audit to monthly growth

  1. Audit. We look at your store, ads, tracking and shipping, and list what is broken and what is missing.
  2. Plan. You get a written scope: what we build, in which order, and what we need from you, such as access and assets.
  3. Build and launch. We build on a test store or in draft first, check everything on real phones, then go live.
  4. Run and improve. Weekly checks, WhatsApp updates and a monthly report. We keep fixing what the numbers show.

FAQ

Questions before you start

Which platforms do you work with?

Shopify first, plus WooCommerce, TikTok Shop and Amazon. For ads: Google and Meta. For messages: Klaviyo, Shopify Email and the WhatsApp Business API. For shipping: FedEx and Correos.

Can you take over an existing store or ad account?

Yes. We start with an audit and fix tracking before we change any campaign or theme.

How fast will I see results?

Ads show first data in 7 to 14 days and a clearer picture after about 30 days. Store and automation work shows its effect soon after launch.

Do you guarantee sales?

No. Marketing is testing. We guarantee the work, clear reporting and honest advice about what is and is not working.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. After the setup period, ongoing work runs month to month.

How do we stay in touch?

WhatsApp for day-to-day questions, weekly updates while work is live, and a monthly report.
